Bottom Line

The report is now explicitly tactical. The base case is still constructive, but the best 1-month setup is in quality, defensives, and select industrial exposure rather than broad beta.
  • Healthcare, Utilities, and Consumer Staples carry the best defensive-adjusted scores because they combine stable revisions with lower rates sensitivity.
  • Industrials remain investable, but only selectively: reshoring and infrastructure help, while a renewed tariff cycle can still compress margins.
  • Semiconductors still have upside, but the 1-month score is lower than a 6-month score would be because positioning is crowded and the path is more yield-sensitive.
  • US quality and Europe stay ahead of China on this horizon because near-term policy visibility and earnings stability matter more than deep valuation discounts.
  • The key tactical risk is not recession. It is a fast repricing in yields, USD, or trade policy that narrows market breadth and punishes cyclicals.

What Would Change the Call

These are the shortest-path invalidation checks for a 1-month outlook. If two or more trigger at once, the dashboard should be refreshed.
  • US 10Y yield rising another 20-25 bps would likely drag semiconductors, real estate, and small cyclicals lower.
  • DXY gaining more than 2% in a month would weaken the case for EM and commodity-linked equities.
  • Brent back above $90 would support Energy but work against the broader disinflation and margin-improvement thesis.
  • Fed cut odds dropping below the current soft-landing baseline would likely flatten leadership back into mega-cap defensives and cash proxies.
