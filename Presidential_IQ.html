Estimates use Simonton's "corrected for reliability, ages 18–26" figures where available. These are historiometric estimates, not measured IQ scores. No formal IQ test results exist for most presidents. Modern presidents (Obama, Trump, Biden) are rough estimates based on academic records, career complexity, and linguistic analysis — not from Simonton's original study, which covers only Washington through George W. Bush (42 chief executives).

Roster status (as of September 2026): 45 individuals have served in 47 presidencies. Donald Trump, the incumbent 47th president (second term since January 20, 2025), is already included below; no new scholarly IQ estimate has been published for any post-Bush officeholder.
