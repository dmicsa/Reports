Estimates use Simonton's "corrected for reliability, ages 18–26" figures where available. These are historiometric estimates, not measured IQ scores. No formal IQ test results exist for most presidents. Modern presidents (Obama, Trump, Biden) are rough estimates based on academic records, career complexity, and linguistic analysis — not from Simonton's original study.
