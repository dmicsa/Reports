Method

  • AUM comes from Yahoo Finance fund statistics and is shown in billions of dollars.
  • AR is annualized return, SD is annualized standard deviation, and SR is Sharpe ratio computed from daily adjusted closes over each horizon.
  • Morningstar rating is scraped from public ETF quote pages when available.
  • Lipper rating is wired into the screener and currently comes from ./etf_screener_overrides.json overrides until a stable public source is added.
