Data Sources & Methodology

  • Federal Bureau of Prisons (BOP) — Religious Preference of Inmates, FOIA data 2025. Total inmates: 153,121 (Feb 2026).
    bop.gov — Inmate Religious Preferences
  • Pew Research Center — "Religion in Prisons: A 50-State Survey of Prison Chaplains" (2012). State prison estimates from chaplain surveys.
    pewresearch.org — Religion in Prisons
  • Pew Research Center — "Religious Landscape Study" (2023–2024). US adult religious composition.
    pewresearch.org — Religious Composition
  • US DOJ Office of Inspector General — Audit of BOP Religious Services (March 2020). ~70% of BOP inmates claim a faith.
    justice.gov — OIG
Representation Index: (% of prison population identifying as X) ÷ (% of US adult population identifying as X). An index of 1.0 means exactly proportional. Values above 1.0 indicate over-representation in prison; below 1.0 indicates under-representation.
Caveats: Prison religious affiliation is self-reported and may reflect conversions during incarceration (especially Islam). "No Preference" and "Unknown" inmates (~36% of federal) are excluded from percentage calculations of religious groups. State prison data relies on 2012 chaplain estimates (impressionistic, not official counts). Socioeconomic confounders (poverty, education, geography) strongly influence incarceration rates and are not controlled for here. This chart shows correlation, not causation.
