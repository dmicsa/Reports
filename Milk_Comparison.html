Price Context (2026)

  • USDA AMS Retail Milk Prices (August 2026): U.S. simple average $4.05 per gallon for conventional whole milk, $4.01 for 2% reduced fat, and $5.18 per half gallon for organic whole milk. 2026 city averages range from ~$2.72 (Indianapolis) to ~$5.46 (Chicago).
  • BLS CPI average price (July 2026): fresh whole, fortified milk at $4.31 per gallon in U.S. city average (FRED series APU0000709112).
  • Plant-based milk pricing: the plant-milk price premium has collapsed from ~18% (2021) to an estimated ~2% in 2026 — functional price parity with dairy in many urban markets. Private-label plant milks retail 20–30% below national brands; wholesale ranges ~$0.90–1.60/L (soy), ~$1.20–2.20/L (oat), ~$1.30–2.40/L (almond).
  • Market shares (2026): almond milk leads plant-based purchases (~58%), followed by oat (~21%, fastest-growing) and soy (~13%).

Nutrition Score formula: Composite of 10 sub-scores (each 0–100, then averaged).
Positive: Protein, Calcium, Vitamin D, Vitamin B12, Potassium → higher = better.
Moderate: Fat (optimal ~3–4 g), Calories (optimal ~50–65 kcal), Carbs (optimal ~4–5 g), Sugar (lower = better).
Bonus: Lactose-free scores a small bonus for digestibility (relevant for ~70% of world population).
All values per 100 ml. Animal milks are whole unless noted. Plant milks are unsweetened fortified. Cells are palette-colored (red = low/weak, green = high/strong; reversed for Sugar and distance-based for Energy, Fat, Carbs around their optimum).
