Data Sources & Methodology

  • USDA FoodData Central — cow, goat, sheep, buffalo milk.
    https://fdc.nal.usda.gov/
  • FAO — Milk composition across species (Park & Haenlein, 2006; FAO Animal Production and Health Paper).
    fao.org
  • NIH / PubMed — Equine, camelid, and donkey milk composition studies.
  • Plant milks: USDA + manufacturer data (unsweetened, fortified variants). Actual values vary by brand.
Nutrition Score formula: Composite of 10 sub-scores (each 0–100, then averaged).
Positive: Protein, Calcium, Vitamin D, Vitamin B12, Potassium → higher = better.
Moderate: Fat (optimal ~3–4 g), Calories (optimal ~50–65 kcal), Carbs (optimal ~4–5 g), Sugar (lower = better).
Bonus: Lactose-free scores a small bonus for digestibility (relevant for ~70% of world population).
All values per 100 ml. Animal milks are whole unless noted. Plant milks are unsweetened fortified.
