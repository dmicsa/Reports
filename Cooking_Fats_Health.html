Data Sources & Methodology

  • USDA FoodData Central — nutritional composition per 100 g.
    https://fdc.nal.usda.gov/
  • Smoke points: The Culinary Institute of America / Serious Eats / Wikipedia compilations.
  • Oxidative stability: Rancimat induction times from peer-reviewed lipid chemistry literature.
Health Score formula: Composite of 10 sub-scores (each 0–100, then averaged):
Positive: MUFA%, Omega-3, Vitamin E, Smoke Point, Oxidative Stability → higher = better.
Negative: SFA%, Trans Fat, Omega-6:3 Ratio → lower = better.
Moderate: PUFA% → optimal ~15–25%, penalised if too high (oxidation risk) or too low.
The score is opinionated and reflects a Mediterranean-diet-aligned perspective favouring MUFA-rich, high-stability fats with low omega-6:3 ratios. Individual health needs may vary.
