GDP per capita = nominal GDP / population, in current US dollars. DC excluded from US states (2025: $277,689 — far above any state). Luxembourg & Ireland excluded from the European quintile calculation as extreme outliers ($131.6k–$147.3k) but shown in the distribution and table. European set includes EU-27 + UK, Norway, Switzerland, Iceland. PPP per capita is available for countries only (US is the PPP base, so US nominal = PPP). Nominal growth is the 2024→2025 change in current-dollar GDP per capita.
