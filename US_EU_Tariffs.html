Data Sources & Context

  • US-EU Framework Agreement / Joint Statement (Aug 21, 2025) — US commits to 15% all-inclusive ceiling (higher of MFN or 15%); MFN-only for cork, aircraft/parts, generic pharmaceuticals, chemical precursors (from Sep 1, 2025); autos reduced to 15% combined (from Aug 1, 2025).
    Joint Statement · Federal Register (Sep 25, 2025)
  • US Supreme Court, Learning Resources v. Trump / Trump v. V.O.S. Selections (Feb 20, 2026, 6-3) — IEEPA does not authorize presidential tariffs; reciprocal and fentanyl tariffs invalidated; refunds to be litigated at the CIT.
    Opinion (24-1287) · CRS Legal Sidebar
  • Section 122 surcharge (Feb 24 – Jul 24, 2026) — 10% global import surcharge under the Trade Act of 1974 (150-day statutory limit); expired Jul 24, 2026 with no congressional extension; legality on appeal at the Federal Circuit.
    Proclamation
  • Section 301 forced-labor tariffs (effective Jul 24, 2026) — 10–12.5% on 60 economies including the EU; EU combined MFN+Section 301 rate generally capped at 10%. Also: Section 301 'overcapacity' investigation (EU included) ongoing; Jul 24, 2026 presidential threat of further Section 301 tariffs on the EU over tech-fine disputes.
    USTR · Honigman alert
  • Section 232 tariffs — steel/aluminum 50% (doubled Jun 2025), copper 50% (Proclamation 10962, Aug 2025), autos/parts 15% combined for the EU; pharmaceuticals/semiconductors/lumber actions pending, capped ≤15% for the EU. Unaffected by the IEEPA ruling.
    White House proclamations
  • EU Implementing Regulations (Council final approval Jun 25, 2026) — eliminate remaining EU customs duties on US industrial goods; TRQs/reduced tariffs for US seafood and non-sensitive agricultural products; lobster duty-free (retroactive Aug 1, 2025, to Jul 31, 2030); main regulation applies to end-2029 with safeguard/suspension mechanisms. Implementing Regulations followed the European Parliament's brief Jan 2026 freeze during the Greenland tariff episode (threatened Jan 17, withdrawn Jan 21, 2026).
    Council press release · EC Trade Policy
  • WTO Tariff Profiles — MFN applied rates by HS chapter (baseline table).
    WTO Statistics
  • Tax Foundation / Yale Budget Lab / CRS — analysis of effective US tariff rates 2025–2026.
    taxfoundation.org · Yale Budget Lab · CRS IF13107
Note: Tariff rates shown are weighted averages or representative rates for each product category; actual rates vary by specific HS code / tariff line and by exemption schedule. "EU on US" reflects the EU's 2026 Implementing Regulations (industrial goods 0%) — the EU's retaliatory tariffs (adopted Jul 2025 against €90bn+ of US goods) remain suspended since Aug 5, 2025, with repeated extensions. The US 15% Framework ceiling remains the agreed cap, but the current general rate on most EU goods is ~10% (MFN + Section 301, combined cap); a pending Section 301 overcapacity investigation and the threatened tech-fines tariff could push rates back toward the 15% ceiling. Data vintage: September 10, 2026. Numbers may change as Section 122 litigation and pending Section 232/301 actions develop.
